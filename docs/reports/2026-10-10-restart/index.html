Public display, storage, caching, derived summaries, attribution, post-license retention and model transmission are distinct questions. None of these source lists clears those rights for this project.

What the permission words mean

Public display: can other people see the data on our page? Caching: can we save a local copy so the page can read it later? Retention: how long may we keep that copy, including after a subscription ends? Derived summaries: may we write short factual notes from it? Model transmission: may we send selected facts to another company’s AI? Each answer needs to cover our exact use.

What this evidence can tell us

Reliable boundaries

  • Deterministic firstProviders and explicit rules establish facts, freshness and ranking. A model may only phrase already-selected evidence.
  • Sparse by designAt most two items per entity. Zero is valid. Every article does not become a timeline item.
  • Spoiler safetyResults are hidden unless deliberately revealed. The concepts’ one revealed result is fictional and labelled.
  • Source rights before rankingA working integration is not permission to publish or transmit its data.

Remaining limits

  • Prototype, not client deliveryThese HTML files are exploratory designs. They do not implement native SwiftUI or fetch the API.
  • Frozen synthetic snapshotDates and statuses come from a fixed public-demo shape. Relative freshness is not a live observation.
  • Model quality unprovedM7’s hand-authored evaluation cases prove the recorded verifier behavior, not that a real model is useful or semantically reliable.
  • Provider terms can changeOfficial links and research dates are included. A contract or written permission must settle the exact planned use.
What can wait / facts only you can supply
  • Provider messages: review the unsent drafts. Sending any message requires your explicit approval.
  • Sportradar trial dates: you will need to sign in to the marketplace and check NFL/Golf subscription start/end dates. The current browser was not signed in. Ask the provider about the destruction certificate using the prepared draft; no account dates or administrative closure are confirmed.
  • Paid data cap: the $10–25 hosting envelope excludes sports data. Set a separate maximum after seeing a commercial quote.
  • Host and domain: choose the account and domain only when ready to provision. No service or domain was purchased. Production monitoring and backup/restore must be verified after the host exists.
  • Routine later engineering: cache cleanup, a generated-summary marker and retries can follow the chosen release. Translation is optional. Broad aggregation, social features, notifications, more entities and automatic background refresh are outside the first release.
